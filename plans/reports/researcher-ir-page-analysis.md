# Vietnamese Investor Relations Page Analysis
**Research Date:** March 25, 2026

## Executive Summary

Analyzed IR pages of 5 major Vietnamese companies across different sectors. Key findings:
- **Vingroup** (Real Estate) & **Hòa Phát** (Steel): Compliance-focused, traditional structures
- **Vinamilk** (Dairy) & **Techcombank** (Banking): Hybrid modern UX with structured data
- **FPT** (Technology): Most advanced storytelling with interactive elements

All companies follow Vietnamese Securities Law requirements (Circular 96/2020, Law 54/2019). English-Vietnamese bilingual disclosure mandatory since Jan 2025.

## Vietnamese Regulatory Compliance Requirements

### Mandatory IR Page Sections (UBCKNN/SSC Requirements)

Per **Law on Securities 54/2019** (amended by Law 56/2024) + **Circular 96/2020/TT-BTC**:

**Periodic Disclosure (Annual/Interim):**
1. **Audited Financial Statements**
   - Consolidated balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement
   - Format: Both VAS (Vietnamese) and IFRS (International)
   - Timing: Full audited within 90 days of fiscal year-end

2. **Management Discussion & Analysis (MD&A)**
   - Financial performance analysis
   - Risk factors and strategy
   - Material changes and future outlook

3. **Annual Report**
   - Comprehensive business overview
   - Financial results and analysis
   - Operational performance by segment
   - Risk management and controls

4. **Corporate Governance Report**
   - Board composition and qualifications
   - Executive compensation disclosure
   - Internal audit and control mechanisms
   - ESG metrics (emissions, energy, water, compliance)
   - Filing deadline: Within 30 days of H1 and year-end

5. **General Shareholder Meeting Documents**
   - Meeting notices and agendas
   - Board resolutions
   - Shareholder voting results
   - Minutes and decisions

6. **Information Disclosure Documents**
   - Related party transactions
   - Major contracts and agreements
   - Regulatory announcements
   - Extraordinary events (24-hour disclosure requirement)

### Language Requirements (Effective Jan 2025)
- All disclosures must be provided in **both English and Vietnamese**
- This explains Vingroup, Vinamilk, Techcombank, and FPT's bilingual IR pages

### Major Shareholder Disclosure Thresholds
- Mandatory disclosure when shareholding ≥ 5% of voting shares
- Must report changes in major shareholder status

### Timeline Compliance
- Extraordinary disclosures: Within 24 hours
- Annual reports: Within 90 days of fiscal year-end
- Shareholder meeting notices: Minimum 20 days advance notice
- Corporate governance reports: Within 30 days of H1 and year-end

## Key Design Insights for AletechWeb

### Structure Recommendations
1. **Mandatory Sections** (Compliance):
   - Financial Statements (VAS + IFRS if applicable)
   - Annual/Interim Reports with MD&A
   - Corporate Governance Report
   - Shareholder Meeting Documents
   - Information Disclosure Archive

2. **Modern UX Best Practices** (from Techcombank + FPT):
   - Card-based metric presentation for financial highlights
   - Event calendar for investor activities
   - Tab-based filtering (by year, report type, language)
   - Mobile-first responsive design
   - Direct PDF + HTML options for documents

3. **Storytelling Elements** (from FPT):
   - Quarterly performance news feed
   - YoY metrics highlighting in cards
   - Company positioning narrative
   - Awards/recognition display (if applicable)
   - Direct IR contact accessibility

4. **Navigation Architecture**:
   - Main hub page with overview metrics
   - Language toggle (EN/VI) prominent
   - Sub-sections: Reports, Governance, Meetings, Disclosures
   - Search + filter functionality
   - Breadcrumb navigation
   - Mobile hamburger menu

### Visual Style Guidance
- **Color**: Avoid multiple brand colors; use 2-3 primary + neutral palette (FPT/Techcombank example)
- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif with strong hierarchy
- **Cards**: Use consistently for metrics, documents, news items
- **Spacing**: Generous white space (Vinamilk/Techcombank approach)
- **Icons**: Minimal, functional (document types, categories)
- **Photography**: Professional but not excessive (FPT balance)

### Data Presentation Priorities
1. **Financial Highlights**: Card-based KPIs (not tables) on main page
2. **Document Access**: Tab system (by type: Annual/Interim/Governance)
3. **Events Calendar**: Clear scheduling with investor event types
4. **News Feed**: Quarterly updates with embedded metrics
5. **Historical Data**: Year-based filtering/archive (not chronological scrolling)
