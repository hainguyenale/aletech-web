## Legal Requirement Compliance

### 3 Key Laws Analyzed

| Law | Requirement | Compliance |
|---|---|---|
| **Thông tư 96/2020/TT-BTC** | Public disclosure of material information | ✓ All 10 companies |
| **Luật Chứng khoán 54/2019/QH14** | Ch. VIII: Financial status, management info, governance | ✓ Statutory minimum; 70% enhanced |
| **Nghị định 155/2020/NĐ-CP** | Capital disclosure, corporate governance, accuracy | ✓ Basic compliance; enhanced by banks |

**Result:** All 10 companies meet statutory minimum requirements.

## Document Gaps (Legal ≠ Published)

These documents are **legally required but rarely published** on IR pages:
- Company Charter (Điều lệ) - 9/10 not published
- Governance Regulations (Quy chế quản trị) - 9/10 not published
- Prospectus (Bản cáo bạch) - 10/10 not published
- Most Board Resolutions (Nghị quyết HĐQT) - 9/10 not published

**Why?** These are filed with authorities but not always publicized. Vietstock Finance & government portals serve as primary archives.
